# Online Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing online retail sales performance using Excel, PostgreSQL, and Power BI.

The project analyzes 541,909 retail transaction records to identify sales trends, customer behavior, product performance, and business opportunities.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Excel:** Data cleaning and preparation
- **PostgreSQL:** Data analysis using SQL
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and visualization
- **DAX:** Measures and time intelligence calculations

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
- Total Revenue, Orders, Customers and Average Order Value
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Monthly Order Trends
- Top Countries by Revenue
- Top Products by Revenue

### 2. Customer Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Repeat vs One-Time Customers
- Top Customers by Revenue
- Customer Purchase Frequency

### 3. Product Performance
- Top Products by Revenue
- Top Products by Quantity Sold
- Product Revenue Contribution
- Average Selling Price by Product

## Key Business Insights
- Total Revenue: **£10.67 Million**
- Total Orders: **19,960**
- Unique Customers: **4,338**
- Average Order Value: **£534.40**
- United Kingdom contributes approximately **84.61%** of total revenue.
- November 2011 recorded the highest monthly revenue.
